# Source dossier: food-lot disposition after a no-detection result

Retrieved 2026-10-07. These FDA sources establish the food-safety process
context. They do not supply the fictional lot's probabilities, utilities, or
information costs.

## Preventive controls and disposition

FDA's HACCP guidance emphasizes hazard analysis, critical controls, monitoring,
corrective action, verification, and records. It explains that microbiological
testing generally plays a verification role rather than replacing preventive
control, and that corrective action after a deviation includes determining the
disposition of affected product.

- [HACCP Principles and Application Guidelines](https://www.fda.gov/food/hazard-analysis-critical-control-point-haccp/haccp-principles-application-guidelines)
- [FSMA Final Rule for Preventive Controls for Human Food](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-preventive-controls-human-food)

This motivates treating process and traceability records as a possible
information source rather than making endpoint testing the only route to a
decision.

## Maintaining control while testing

FDA's Salmonella-testing guidance recommends maintaining control of food being
tested until the final outcome, so it remains possible to recondition, destroy,
or divert the food. It also says that a confirmed positive should generally be
treated as valid even if later testing of the original or other samples is
negative.

- [Guidance for Industry: Testing for *Salmonella* Species in Human Foods and
  Direct-Human-Contact Animal Foods](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidance-industry-testing-salmonella-species-human-foods-and-direct-human-contact-animal-foods)

The fictional lot therefore remains held throughout information acquisition.
Recall is outside this example because no product has entered distribution.

## Laboratory-method context

FDA's Bacteriological Analytical Manual (BAM) publishes preferred laboratory
procedures for microbiological analysis of foods and cosmetics, including
sampling and preparation and a current Salmonella chapter.

- [Bacteriological Analytical Manual](https://www.fda.gov/food/laboratory-methods-food/bacteriological-analytical-manual-bam)
- [BAM Chapter 5: Salmonella](https://www.fda.gov/food/laboratory-methods-food/bam-chapter-5-salmonella)

This cookbook fixture does not implement, validate, or claim conformance with a
BAM method or regulatory sampling plan.

## Boundary of the cookbook fixture

SP-204, the dry-spice product, facility, initial screen, process history, state
probabilities, sampling likelihoods, utilities, and costs are all synthetic.
The prior is stated **after** a fictional initial no-detection screen; BKM is not
asked to reconstruct that update without the necessary sampling model.

The states distinguish safe or below concern, localized contamination, and
widespread contamination. Localized contamination has a substantial chance of
producing no detection in the fabricated sampling models. This is how the
fixture represents the central lesson: a result is evidence about sampled
material under a model, not proof that every unit in a heterogeneous lot is
safe.

The numbers were chosen to make an inspectable teaching result:

1. continued hold is preferred under current information;
2. all three investigations have positive net information value;
3. confirmatory culture has the greatest gross information value; and
4. the lower-cost process-record investigation has the greatest net value.

Nothing here is a regulatory standard, food-safety determination, sampling
plan, recall decision, or recommendation for a real product.
